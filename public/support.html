SUPPORT

How can we help?

For account access, data corrections or operational support, contact your authorised Voxel Veda administrator. Never include a password, access token, banking password or sensitive identity document in a support message.

Account access

Staff and customers should ask their administrator to confirm the account status, email address and assigned permissions.

Commercial enquiries

New customers can submit a structured request through the RFQ form.

Privacy or security concern

Report suspected unauthorised access promptly through your company contact channel and preserve relevant times, screenshots and device details.
